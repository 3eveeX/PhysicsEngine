This is a physics engine made pretty much entirely in c++.

You can edit the bodies, springs, effectors, and general world settings in the GUI on the left of the screen.

Types of effectors:
 - Point
 - Area
 - Gravity
 - Drag

Types of Bodies:
 - Static
 - Dynamic
 - Kinematic

To add bodies, simply left click in the field. To Spawn several at once, hold left click while holding ctrl. To attach springs between bodies, right click on a body, drag to the body you wish to connect it to while holding right click, and release on the body you are connecting the first body to via springs.
